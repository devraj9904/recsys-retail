recsys-retail

Live recommendations for RetailRocket shoppers. Click View, Cart or Buy on any item and the list re-ranks with that click included.

  1. Memory. Every click is stored in Redis: the visitor's last 90 events, session and favourite categories.
  2. Taste profile. A neural network (the user tower of a two-tower model) turns that history into 64 numbers. Every product has 64 numbers too, learned from millions of past clicks.
  3. Shortlist (~120). The 100 products closest to the visitor's profile (FAISS search over 148k items), plus items they already touched, plus this week's trending items.
  4. Ranking. 24 clues per product (how often and how recently they touched it, popularity, how often viewers buy it, category and price fit) feed two LightGBM models: one for engagement, one for purchase intent (add-to-cart or purchase). Their scores are blended.
  5. Variety. MMR swaps near-duplicates for different items, then the top 12 are shown.
  6. New visitors. No clicks: trending items. Clicks only on items the models never saw: products with similar details (content similarity).
